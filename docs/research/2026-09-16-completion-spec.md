## Product and trust requirements

### Access and provider ownership

- Public users can sign up, authenticate, recover access and create private workspaces. Bootstrap membership server-side and atomically; a caller cannot nominate another user's organization or acquire a role from editable metadata. Preserve existing owner/editor/reviewer/viewer authorization, database membership checks and forced tenant isolation.
- AI access is bring-your-own-key (BYOK), OpenRouter first; direct providers follow through the same narrow adapter contract. Product selection does not waive the provider-change ADR, evidence or compatibility review. Provider/model routes are pinned and observable, not silently substituted.
- A missing, revoked, invalid, exhausted or unauthorized user credential stops that user's billable work with a typed actionable error. There is no application-owner key fallback, cross-user key borrowing, or undisclosed provider/model downgrade. Workspace membership does not itself grant access to another member's key.
- Credentials enter through an approved authenticated secret-management flow. Store only encrypted secret material/references server-side; never return plaintext after capture or expose it in logs, prompts, traces, analytics, fixtures, exports or issue/PR text. Support validation, replacement and revocation, metadata-only status and ownership authorization. Queued jobs must recheck credential authorization and revocation before provider use. The credential schema, encryption/key-management mechanism and job binding are frozen in the contract slice before implementation.
- Preserve cost ceilings, pre-run estimates/approval, usage accounting, bounded retries, timeouts and explicit failures. BYOK changes payer identity, not safety or budget enforcement. Embeddings and generation capabilities remain separately validated; an OpenRouter generation route is not evidence of an embeddings capability.

### Forecasting, audit and exports

Freeze fit/predict/backtest and immutable admitted request/result/point contracts before implementation. Requests pin graph/scenario, target/unit, fiscal horizon, cutoff, dataset/corpus, training window, feature vintages, algorithm/configuration and budget. Queue/lease state stays separate. Results/points publish atomically once; failed/abstained runs cannot expose successful partial forecasts.

Targets are quarterly revenue, ARR where disclosed and gross profit; horizons 1–8 quarters, with 1–4 and 5–8 reported separately. Use ≥12 available quarters where sufficient; otherwise abstain or explicitly disclose shorter history. Implement last-value, seasonal-naive and analyst-driver lanes, and the parent specification's registered statistical/ensemble capabilities. LLMs may explain/propose assumptions, never write authoritative forecast values. Missing required publication timestamps or market adjustments fails closed.

Rolling/expanding backtests freeze actual available vintages at every origin, including corrected data availability. Produce 50/80/95% intervals with method/version and target/horizon calibration. Advanced defaults require better median MAE than seasonal-naive over supported 1–4 horizons; otherwise baseline stays default. **Accepted execution proposal, requiring formal contract freeze before dispatch:** ETS with additive damped trend and additive seasonality is the statistical challenger; use an equal-weight ensemble of approved models. The statistical lane abstains below 12 quarters. Historical rolling-origin residual intervals use finite conformal ranks and at least 19 observations for the same issuer/target/horizon, with no implicit pooling. Insufficient support produces an explicit unavailable interval/abstention according to the frozen result contract. These algorithm/minimum choices require validation and do not establish empirical coverage. Do not infer calibrated intervals from point predictions. Historical-analogue forecast and Atlas remain deferred.

Audit traversal preserves source→span→approved extraction→assumption/formula/model→forecast→claim→export relationships. Export Markdown/PDF briefs, CSV/XLSX tables, JSON evidence bundles and reproducible workspace manifests. Distinguish reported, derived, user-supplied and forecast values. Verify bytes/hashes and every required typed provenance edge before publishing an immutable bundle. Regeneration creates a new bundle; content hashes do not grant access. Use existing private object storage with authorized expiring access.
